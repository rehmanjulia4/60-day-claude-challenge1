60-Day Claude AI Challenge · Skills Evolution

Sixty Days,
One Ascent.

Every stage below was built in public, one deliverable at a time. The capstone at the summit, HealthNexus Command Center v1.0.0, is what all ten stages were actually for.

01
Days 1–6

AI Foundations

Core mechanics of working with Claude: framing tasks, reading model output critically, and the first daily-deliverable discipline that would hold for sixty straight days.

02
Days 7–12

Effective Prompting

Moving from asking questions to specifying outcomes: constraints, formats, and the reframing habit, generic prompt in, healthcare-executive artifact out, that shaped the rest of the challenge.

03
Days 13–18

Reasoning Workflows

Multi-step problem decomposition, structured critique loops, and treating Claude's first draft as a starting position to interrogate rather than a finished answer.

04
Days 19–24

Research Workflows

Sourcing real, citable data instead of accepting plausible-sounding numbers, the principle that would later become the hard "no fabricated statistics" rule across every HealthNexus tool.

05
Days 25–30

Automation

Repeatable pipelines instead of one-off outputs: the earliest version of the standardized deliverable pipeline (tool, carousel, docs, post) that every later day would reuse.

06
Days 31–36

Product Thinking

Naming the actual user, CDO, CMIO, COO, CNO, and building for their decisions and constraints instead of a generic "healthcare professional."

07
Days 37–42

Increasingly Sophisticated AI Projects

Multi-screen tools with real state, not single-page demos, plus the first fixed LinkedIn and carousel formats that would carry through to Day 60.

08
Days 43–47

Multimodal AI

Bringing image generation, screenshot-driven carousels, and visual QA into the daily pipeline, laying the groundwork for HealthNexus's three-theme, bilingual design system.

09
Days 48–50

Knowledge Systems

Structured, queryable information design, the direct precursor to schema.js and incidentState.js as governance boundaries in the capstone.

10
Days 51–59

Autonomous Agent & Multi-Agent Architecture

Ten days building HealthNexus Command Center: a no-bundler React build, a mandatory clinician sign-off boundary, three themes, true bilingual RTL, and a Day 58 hardening pass that caught four real production bugs.

★
Day 60

HealthNexus Command Center

v1.0.0 Shipped

A bilingual, three-themed, offline-deployable clinical incident triage dashboard where every AI recommendation requires named clinician sign-off before action. Not a demo of governance, an architecture that enforces it. This is what fifty-nine days of building toward something pointed at, on purpose.

Dr. Julia Rehman · HealthNexus
