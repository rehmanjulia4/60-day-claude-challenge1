Day 44 / 60 🩺

My own LinkedIn scored 54/100 on a healthcare executive search lens I built today. Eighteen years running enterprise analytics and clinical informatics across four countries, and my Skills section had exactly three keywords, none of them "data."

So today's build isn't a generic LinkedIn optimizer. It's a healthcare-specific positioning analyzer that scores profiles the way a CDO or CMIO search committee actually reads them, then applies it to two scenarios: my own real profile, and a synthetic composite physician moving toward her first leadership role, whose strongest leadership evidence (chairing a quality committee) was written into her About section as a hobby.

01. A LinkedIn skills section with only 3 keywords is invisible to search, regardless of how strong the actual career is.
02. Governance and committee work reads as leadership signal only when it is named as leadership, not buried as a bullet point.
03. Clinicians transitioning to leadership need translation, not more credentials: the leadership experience is often already there, just not labeled as such.

If you had to guess your own profile's weakest section before running it through a tool, which one would it be?
