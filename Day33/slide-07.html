Challenge 2 \u00b7 The Reveal
Target audience

General public frustrated with healthcare costs

Manipulation technique

Outrage bait + single anecdote generalized to "the system"

A neutral, informative rewrite

"One family reported a $40,000 bill for a visit they expected to be routine. Billing complexity is a documented, systemic issue \u2014 though one bill does not describe every hospital."

Key takeaway

A real, sympathetic anecdote can still generalize unfairly. Ask what evidence supports "the system," not just the one story.
