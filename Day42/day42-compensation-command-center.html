Decision Support
AI-generated planning notes
Cash flow

A 0% local tax market produces a materially higher net take-home before any housing allowance is even included, but always compare net-to-net, not gross-to-gross.

Vesting risk

A US equity grant with a one-year cliff means leaving before month twelve forfeits the entire tranche.

Retirement

GCC end-of-service formulas often accelerate after year five, rewarding longer tenure differently than a typical US employer retirement match.

Filing complexity

A move to a 0%-tax country does not remove US filing obligations for citizens; budget for a cross-border tax advisor in year one.

Before You Sign
Offer evaluation checklist
Confirm whether the housing allowance is guaranteed cash or employer-provided housing
Verify the EOSB or retirement calculation basis: basic salary only, or full gross package
Confirm equity refresh policy and cliff terms in writing before accepting
Engage a cross-border tax advisor if relocating as a US citizen
Model relocation, schooling, and healthcare costs separately from base comparison
