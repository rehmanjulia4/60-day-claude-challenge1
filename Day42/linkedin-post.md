Every healthcare executive comparing a US role to a GCC role hits the same trap: comparing headline numbers instead of net numbers. A 0% UAE tax rate looks like the obvious win, until you remember a US citizen abroad still owes the IRS on worldwide income. And it's not just one country pair, Texas has no state income tax either, so two "0% local tax" offers can still land in very different places.

Today's prompt was a generic Personal Financial Command Center. I rebuilt it as an Executive Compensation & Equity Command Center: pick any two markets, 11 US states and all six GCC countries, and compare the same target package on one ledger. Real 2025 state tax rates, real MERIC and Numbeo cost-of-living data, and each GCC country's actual gratuity law, all cited on the page. Bilingual EN/AR, full RTL, three executive themes, and a 15-step screenshot walkthrough of the tool actually running.

01. A market picker beats a fixed pair once the tax and gratuity models are built generically.
02. Two cost-of-living indices with different baselines can't be blended into one honest number.
03. Screenshotting every screen for the walkthrough, not just the happy path, caught a real bug: the print view was leaving dark panels on an otherwise white page. Worth testing the parts nobody clicks.
